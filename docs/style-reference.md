## Translator traps and recommended approaches
- **おまえ, Grineed's trigger** (ending 1A6D6C-1A7750; Rozzgoat's backstory in ev_20). English "you" is not rude, so
  give Beet one disrespectful word Grineed can spell back: "buddy". Beet: "I'm gonna beat you, buddy!"; Grineed:
  "...Bud..." / "...dy..." / "...'Buddy'?"; scream "'BUDDY'?!"; Beet: "Yeah. I'm gonna beat you, BUDDY!".
  Rozzgoat: "I once called Grineed-sama 'buddy'." Same word in every line.
- **凶暴, the second trigger** (1BF4C4-1BF618): "savage", echoed "Sav... age...?" then "I am intelligent and COOL!"
- **ＢＢ = ボインボイン** (189C1A-18B2EC): the old man reads BB as "Boing-Boing" (kid-safe, keeps both B's). Milfa:
  "BB means Broad Buster!" Punchline 「おお！ＢＢとは！確かにＢＢだっ！」: "Oh! So that's BB! She's BB, all right!"
  repeated word for word by Milfa and mimicked by Poala.
- **Mountain Hermit riddles** (ev_21, quoted in ev_24 24C966/24CA42/24CB34): one fixed wording each, reused
  exactly; keep the clue words the characters quote back.
  1. 高き頂きにたたずむ者、その頂点 -> "One who stands upon the high summit: its pinnacle." (answer: the Etria
     Mountains, 3200)
  2. 大地にこしをすえかがやきをさえぎる者、そのきわみ -> "One who sits rooted in the earth and blocks the radiance:
     its utmost." (the Duora Plains tree; girths as "hugs around")
  3. ふきぬけとどまらざる者、その優劣 -> "One who blows through and never stays: the mightiest of them." (the Musley
     Wasteland)
  ナゾかけ (the Hermit's word) = "conundrum"; なぞなぞ = "riddle"; Kissu's gloss: "A 'conundrum'... a riddle, basically."
- **やけっぱち** (1A7C20-1A7C86): "If it's just setting myself on fire, even I can do that!" ... [PAGE] "This is me
  going down in flames... on purpose!"
