- ev:198854 - この子の想い・・・この子をころしてはいけない - "this boy" twice in a row is clunky in English. - Yes… his feelings….!! We can't let this boy die…!
- ev:1992D2 - はっ・・・！オレの・・・命・・・みんなの才牙が・・・ "Ah...! My life... everyone's Saiga..." - here はっ is a gasp of realisation, not "Huh". The trailing "are…" also dangles. - Ah…! My… life… it's everybody's Saiga…
- ev:19A3DE - なるほどなっ！わかったぜ！ "Makes sense! Got it!" - "Gotcha! Alright, I got it!" says the same thing twice. - Makes sense! Got it!
- ev:19D1CA - 死闘 "life-or-death battle" - "death match" sounds like wrestling. - …the life-or-death battle between the Zenon Warriors, the continent's strongest, and Beltorze, the King of Tragedy…
- ev:19DE92 - 5人の、5つの才牙を具現化できるかも "you may be able to materialize the five Saiga of the five" - "all five Saiga of the five of them" is hard to read. - …In other words, not just that Burning Lance… you may be able to materialize all five of their Saiga…!!
- ev:19E5E0 - では、いきますよ！ (start of the Saiga practice) - Juno's matching cue 19A4BA is "Here we go." "Here I come" says Horus himself is the opponent. Use it only if he really is. - Here we go, then!
- ev:19E69E - 後はいろんなところで経験を重ねて、りっぱな・・・ゼノン戦士団の思いをつげるようなりっぱなバスターに - the self-correction doesn't flow ("The rest is to gain experience… and become a fine… I hope you become a fine Buster"). - …From here on, gain experience in all sorts of places and become a fine… a fine Buster who can carry on the Zenon Warriors' will.
