# Review r00 (b002-b005)

### Issues

#### Critical
(none)

#### Important
- ev:178352 - 変なお兄ちゃんが・・・ボール取ったぁ (a weird older boy took my ball) - "A weird big brother" reads as the kid's own sibling; お兄ちゃん here is a stranger - "Some weird big kid... took my baaaall!! Waaaaaaah!!"

#### Minor
- ev:171EB8 - しかしトラキラの葉はもう持てないのであきらめた - breaks the fixed pattern every other "can't carry" line uses (and adds "You found") - "But you can't carry any more Torakira Leaves, so you left it."
- ev:174136 - 井戸からくんでおる (old man, おる) - "you know" is neutral; old-man register thin - "All the water this village uses is drawn from the well east of the village, I'll have you know." (or "...east of here, eh.")
- ev:174890 - バスターのお兄ちゃんだ - "the Buster Mister" is awkward - "Hey, it's Mister Buster! Come play with me!"
- ev:172ED8 - 調査の方はすすんでるかしら (Orfina) - trailing "I wonder?" on a question reads stiff - "I wonder how your investigation is coming along?"
- ev:177730 - よろしくたのまぁ (Badu, on joining) - means "glad to work with ya / counting on ya", not "count on me" - "Glad to be aboard, pal." (same for Shiba ev:180982 よろしくたのむよ: "Glad to have you, then.")
- ev:17AFB8 - 楽しみにしていてね (Wes) - "you hear?" is rustic, off for the polite scientist - "Look forward to the day the black swamps disappear, okay?"
- ev:17C0C2 - あたしに・・・何か用かい？ - "You want... something with me?" unidiomatic - "You want... something from me?"
- ev:17E300 - バスターをやめて (Riddle Master) - "quit Busting" coins an odd verb - "how would you like to quit being Busters and carry the next generation of the riddle world on your shoulders?!"
- ev:181348 - 兵士達にすれば彼らがいるのに (when they, the soldiers, are already here) - "when it already has them" can read as "already has Busters" - "The soldiers hate that the kingdom goes out of its way to hire Busters when it's already got them, the soldiers."  (or "...when it has its own soldiers.")
- ev:181640 - トロワナの墓の調査 - "the Trowana graves" vs the map name Trowana Cemetery players see - "Is your investigation of Trowana Cemetery going well, eh? Now, that aside..."
- ev:17CE52 - さぁ、そこまでは知らないなぁ - "I don't know that much" flat - "Why? Beats me."
