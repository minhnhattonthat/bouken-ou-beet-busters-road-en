# Review r03 - b018-b021

### Issues

#### Critical
None.

#### Important
- ev:1AFA60 - Poala: 「１ＭＧ（マギー）も無いわよ」 "not even 1 MG" - the furigana (マギー) was carried over as words, so the line reads "a single MG of Magi". The style rule says to drop furigana. - "Sorry, but I don't have a single MG to give you Kanecks! I'm already in this mess thanks to my idiot partner... Honestly!!"
- ev:1AFAFC - Beet: 「バカって・・・・・・」 is Beet repeating, hurt, what Poala just called him ("Idiot, she says..."). The bare "Idiot......" reads as Beet calling Poala an idiot, so the addressee flips. - "'Idiot'......?" or "Idiot, she says......"

#### Minor
- ev:1AD3F8 - Poala's Mom: 「一度言い出したらきかない」 "once you've said something, you won't be talked out of it". "once you say something, you never listen" is clumsy. - "Ever since you were little, once you've made up your mind, there's no talking you out of it..."
- ev:1AE162 - Mugine: 「ヴァンデルのプライドというもの」. "Even I have a thing called Vandel pride" is stiff. - "...Even I have my pride as a Vandel, you see..."
- ev:1AE466 - Mugine: 「苦しまぎれの天撃も　はずれたなァ」. Here も is emphatic ("even your desperate shot"). "missed, too" suggests something else also missed. - "Ha ha ha! Even your desperate Divine Attack missed!!"
- ev:1AF990 - Poala: 「野宿にねぶそく」 "camping out plus no sleep". "from sleeping outside with no sleep" contradicts itself. - "I'm already cranky enough from camping out and getting no sleep!!"
- ev:1B1DA8 - Haia: 「それで依頼完了、とさせて下さい」. "Please let that complete the request." is awkward. - "...we'll consider the request complete."
- ev:1B1F8A - Haia: 「これで里もひと安心、というところです」 "I'd say the village can rest easy now". "as they say" is added and implies an idiom that isn't there. - "Now the village can rest a little easier, I'd say."
- ev:1B2782 / ev:1B2A1E - 大気を練る (the Divine Attack mechanic). It is "Knead the air" here, but Horus says "refined air" (ev_19) and ev_1C 2698 says "draw in the air's power". In the same scene 練ってる became "charging". Pick one form for the glossary, for example "focus the air", and apply it everywhere. - "Focus the air properly, like you mean it...!!"
- ev:1B28EA - Poala: 「ウソでしょーーーーーっ！？」. Per the style rule ！？ -> ?!, but the EN has "!?". - "No way... You've GOT to be kiddiiing?!"
- ev:1B2C0E - Poala: 「でっ・・・でかっ！！」 "s-so big!!". "what a monster" loses the size. - "Wh-whoa, it's HUGE!!"
- ev:1B3546 - Gate: 「ぜひお前たちの力をかりたい」 "I must ask for your strength". "I would gladly borrow" sounds as if the Gate is doing them a favour. - "To break it, I must ask to borrow your strength."
- ev:1B49BE - Beet: 「ポアラのおっちゃん」. "Your old man" is fine slang, but elsewhere Beet calls him "Uncle". - "Your dad's always looked out for me too,"
- ev:1B57F6 - Beet: "I'm not bein' a Buster 'cause I want rewards!!" is clunky. - "Don't sweat it! I'm not a Buster for the rewards!!"
- ev:1B6CEE / ev:1B6E20 - Wes: 「ビィト君たち」 "Beet-kun and co.". The EN addresses only "Beet-kun", which drops Poala. - "So I'd like to ask you two, Beet-kun, to escort them on the way back." / "So, Beet-kun, once you two are ready, please head to the Coast Ruins."

### Assessment

- b019: Needs fixes - 1AFA60 has the furigana Magi carried into the line, and the addressee of 1AFAFC is ambiguous. The Athos and Haia voices are good.
- b020: Approved - minor only. The "That's what they say" joke and the gag "future wife" / "lovers' quarrel" land.
- b021: Approved - minor only. The proverb matches the style reference.
