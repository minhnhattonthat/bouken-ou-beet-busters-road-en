# Synopsis - Bouken-Ou Beet: Busters Road

Plot in PLAY order, merged from the recon reports (work/tl/recon/r1-r5.md). File order is not story order: ev_19 from
195830 is the opening; ev_1A holds the prologue, then the ending and credits, then the start of the main game three
years later; ev_17 is per-map NPC data (all chapters); ev_24 is the quest board (titles + descriptions, in plot order).
REVEAL marks a fact the player learns at that point: never state or hint it in an earlier line.
Names follow glossary.md and work/tl/recon/glossary_draft.md.

## Overview
The Dark Century: Vandels (demons) rule with hordes of monsters; Vandel Busters hunt them for bounties. Beet, a cocky
rookie Buster from Anklus Village, sees the Zenon Warriors (his idol Zenon and four comrades) fall to Beltorze; dying,
they pour their lives into five Saiga that now live in Beet. Three years later Beet (15) sets out with Poala to end the
Dark Century. Most of the game is the fight against the Vandel Grineed, "the Verdant Strategist", who has sunk the
Black Horizon (the land beyond the Redou River, around the Kingdom of Trowana) in black smoke for 20+ years. Along
the way Slade, Kissu and Milfa join; Kissu turns out to have served Grineed. It ends at Grineed Castle, with the first
sunlight on the Black Horizon. Tone: shonen comedy with real grief at the deaths (the Zenon Warriors, Alford).

## Chapters in play order

### 1. Opening: the fall of the Zenon Warriors (ev_19 195830-19FFE8)
- Places: Anklus Village, Anklus Plains, Unorom Village. Beet is about 12.
- Text crawl "The Dark Century" (195830-195B62). The Appraiser (old woman) stamps the painful Buster contract on Beet.
- The Zenon Warriors (Zenon, Laio, Cruss, Bluezam, Alside), hired by Anklus, kill 30 Iron Rhinos at the talking Gate.
  Villagers fear Beltorze, "King of Tragedy". Beet demands to join; Poala drags him off; Beet's "that's called
  justice" speech (197290, recalled by Zenon at 198802).
- Beltorze attacks; the five draw their Saiga; he wants their heads for a Vandel "star". Phantom Explosion; Beet is
  dying. The five put their remaining life into their Saiga and into Beet, and vanish. REVEAL: Zenon is Beet's elder
  brother (he left him in the village as a baby). Beet vows to end the Dark Century and cries one last time.
- Tutorials: Juno (199D72-, controls, Rotation Menu, Skill Setup screen); Horus at Unorom explains Divine Power /
  Divine Attack / Saiga. REVEAL: Beet's lance is Laio's Burning Lance; Horus guesses all five Saiga live in Beet.
- The Gate lets Beet out (19EA76-); first quests (19EC08-19F5C4): clear the Anklus Plains, patrol Primore, Elfast,
  Insua.

### 2. Prologue: rookie Beet (ev_1A 1A0016-1A61AC)
- The Appraiser keeps forbidding risky jobs; Beet takes them: Aqua Dog in the water-source pond; the village festival
  (Elder Debre, youth Fural, oak branches; boss: giant bee); Icicle Bats in a Wandara Mountains cave, watched by a ???.
- Shaderico, "Fang of Darkness" (bat Vandel), attacks Unorom. Horus is badly hurt shielding Beet from his Dark
  Attack. Beet tracks him to the bat cave and drives him off alone. Horus: Beet inherited the Zenon Warriors' power.
- Beet leaves on a training journey. Card 「そして３年後・・・」 (1A61AC).

### 3. Three years later: Poala and Mugine (ev_1A ~1AAB60-1AFF96)
- Places: Anklus Village and Plains, Mugine's Dead Swamp, Mugine Castle.
- Poala is now a Buster and has gone after Mugine, a two-star Vandel who spread 84 black swamps. Beet saves her from
  Mugine and his Drawmen. Her father was crippled by a Mud Lizard; Beet clears the Plains swamps overnight.
- Mugine ambushes Beet at the village Gate; Poala's Hail Shot saves him. Boss: Mugine. Poala joins as "No. 2 of the
  Beet Warriors". First camp; Kanecks eat Poala's money (running gag). REVEAL to Poala: the five Saiga in Beet.

### 4. Elfast to Redou: Slade, Shaderico, Grineed (ev_1B 1B003C-1BF67C)
- Athos the Armorer (Elfast, Beetle Shell -> Athos Shield); Elder Haia (Insua, 5 monster drops); Jagarm hunt.
  REVEAL: Beet cannot use Divine Attack at all. Boss: Giant Beetle.
- Primore: the talking Gate knows Beet; Beet calls Poala his future wife (running gag). Wes, black-swamp researcher:
  4 swamp samples, 4 body fluids, escort the White Sand transport from the Coast Ruins (the "growl" is a hole in the
  wall).
- Redou: Slade has taken Beet's job. Spear duel record 41-40-8 -> 41-41-8; Beet and Poala join Slade's job.
  Shaderico (Wandara Mountains) dies. Diner: waitress Minny shuns Busters; Beet quotes Zenon on justice.
- Night raid on Redou; Dangol shows a captured Poala; Sinner Torches. A hidden archer shoots Beet (he caught the
  arrow). REVEAL: the archer is Grineed, "the Verdant Strategist", who culls rookies so rivals can't farm stars.
  Beet calls him savage (凶暴) and Grineed snaps (1BF4C4-1BF618).

### 5. Beltorze's Phantom; Redou quests (ev_1C 1C035A-1CFF9E)
- Beltorze (first as ???) takes over from Grineed and reveals Grineed only poses as an intellectual. Beet: "You
  killed me." Boss: Beltorze; Zenon Winzard is "mere imitation". REVEAL (Poala, from her father): Zenon is Beet's
  real brother. Poala is slowly poisoned by Grineed. Slade holds Beltorze off.
- Redou Appraiser (old man): antidotes ruined; Beet forms Crown Shield and purges the poison. Rematch: Beltorze's
  Phantom Explosion blocked by Crown Shield. REVEAL (1C5772): that Beltorze was a Phantom, a decoy body.
- Grineed's tantrum at Dangol ("Be cool... be cool"); he calls Beet a "Wonder Boy". Redou quests: Wandara remnants
  and wildfire, relief supplies, Gourmet Ant hill, supplies for Mac and Defi (Asad Mountains), flyers at Asad, the
  stolen Sunstone of Redou Lighthouse (keeper Media) -> free ferry tickets.
- Inn talk, two branches (1CEB1A-1CF168 Slade stays a while / 1CF1A2-1CF6D4 Slade leaves); Slade is in the party
  for the next chapters either way (he rejoins via the party menus).

### 6. Across the river: Trowana, Ventura, Frausky (ev_1D 1D0000-1DFFEC)
- Armed passenger ship over the Redou River; Longneck Rays; Poala's "6- and 7-star Vandels" speech.
- Trowana Forest: puppet soldiers; Ventura (3-star, cowardly); Rozzgoat (Grineed's watcher) takes him away. Soldier
  Alford guides them; Black Horizon exposition (no sunlight for 20+ years).
- Kingdom of Trowana gate: Frausky, "the Vivid Crimson Bullet", Grineed's hitman, shoots Alford dead; fight;
  he spares Beet over a hurt little bird. Tavern: Goran punches Beet; Shiba; Busters who never leave the walls.
- Kou's mother Shiho has Ventura's Rot Drug illness; Dr. Yuzu needs the Ameri Herb (Etria Mountains); Ventura drops
  it. Kou gives Poala a flower hairpin.
- Forest Ruins treasure: a Living Coin; a black door with 5 orbs in a constellation; stargazing from Poala's roof in
  Anklus (memory of Bluezam). Colour order red, blue, yellow, green, white.

### 7. The Orb, Terceron, the cemetery (ev_1E 1E0048-1EFFD8)

### 8. Mugine Castle; Kissu returns (ev_1F 1F0050-1FFFFA)
- Ventura's Rot Bug machine in Mugine Castle (Fire Pillar wrecks it); a ??? pulls him out ("looked like someone I

- Orb research: capture the weakest monster of each type alive; at Patres only Penbury reacts (tree-type).

### 9. Kissu's betrayal; three Vandels fall (ev_20 200014-20FFD2)
- Frausky self-destructs; Grineed's lair scene ("he" decoded the ancient Vandel script). Beet, buried, survives
  through Boltic Axe; REVEAL: Cyclone Gunner charges by breathing in sync.

  dies with him). Boss: Rozzgoat (Illusion Mist); his backstory: he called Grineed おまえ and lost an eye.
- Kissu breaks Rozzgoat's Hellfire Dark Torrent. Grineed's bracelet poisons Kissu; Beet cuts it, cures him with
  Crown Shield. Kissu joins the Beet Warriors and cries it out.
- Quests: Cabin's stolen million-MG coin (Kanecks); the Dawn Axe bandits and Gazetta (-> New Dawn Axe); jelly blobs.

### 10. Mountain Hermit; Milfa (ev_21 210042-21FFD2)
- Blob quest ends. The Mountain Hermit blocks the new Asad bridge with three riddles; the Riddle Master (Terceron)
  helps: highest mountain (Etria Mountains, 3200), biggest tree (the Duora Plains tree), strongest wind (Musley
  Wasteland). The Hermit leaves to ponder "human feelings".
- Musley Village destroyed by Dirt Anemones. Milfa the Broad Buster (BB) hunts a rogue Buster (Red Van Sword); Beet
  blocks with Excellion Blade; Milfa adores Zenon; Poala jealous. Boss: giant Dirt Anemone. Milfa comes along.
  Variant meeting 21E318- (see Side content).

### 11. Kissu on probation; Milfa leaves (ev_22 220026-22FA1E)
- Grineed blames and beats Dangol (two versions). Milfa handcuffs Kissu; the team offers to share his punishment;
  Milfa puts him on probation instead.
- Hound Knight pack (Patres). Milfa leaves (two versions). Quests: Triark rebuilding, Crimson Giant, Yo Ore for
  Sadram (pick one partner), Black Horizon plants, Holy Water (Underground Lake; Battleship Tortoise), Patres
  landslide (Volcano Wyvern), caravan kidnapping by Dangol. Grineed begins experiments on the captives.

### 12. Sadram falls; Grineed Castle (ev_23 2300B8-23EB5A)
- Party: Beet, Poala, Kissu, Milfa, Slade (party full -> one must stay out). Sadram is razed by Drawmasters slipped in
  by Helgar, Grineed's pet monster (bosses: Drawmasters, Helgar). Escort the settlers to Trowana.
- Travel Pass from the Appraiser; Grineed Castle in the Musley Mountains: gatekeeper boss, Dark Power seal.
  Upper floor: boss Dangol (seems to die). Jail: the caravan, some driven mad. Monster Lab wrecked "as if by a beast".
  Core: regenerates until Gazetta brings the Forest Orb from the Appraiser.
- Kissu falls through a trap; Grineed wants him back. REVEAL: Kissu's research helped the Rot Bugs, the ruins and the
  Monster Lab; Grineed wrecked the lab in a rage. Kissu: "No." Dangol returns (comic). Boss: Grineed.

### 13. Ending (ev_1A 1A6B6A-1AA462)
- Beet says おまえ to Grineed; he bursts into the Bloodstained Beast. REVEAL (Dangol): Grineed soaked daily in the
  herb Trachia to keep a green skin that calmed him and held down his Dark Power.
- Beet's flame Divine Attack on himself (やけっぱち pun). Grineed, calm again, praises his "Wonder Boy" and dies.
  "Miracle Team". First sunlight on the Black Horizon in over 20 years; the King of Trowana, a soldier at Alford's
  grave. Staff credits 1A9D10-1AA462.

## Who is who
- Beet: 15, rookie-turned-hero Buster from Anklus; carries the five Saiga; can't use Divine Attack.
- Poala: Beet's childhood friend, Divine Attack user (Flame, Fire Pillar, Hail Shot); "No. 2 of the Beet Warriors".
- Slade: rival Buster, mercenary pro, Silent Grave; joins at Redou, comes and goes.
- Kissu: Beet's friend from 2 years ago, Divine Attack genius; secretly served Grineed (REVEAL ev_20).
- ？？？？ (masked boy, ev_1E-1F): Kissu in secret (REVEAL ev_20); speaks with ボク, serves "His Excellency". Tag
  "???"; never name him or hint his name in those lines. ？？？？ also tags Shaderico, Beltorze, Dangol, Grineed and
  Slade before each is named.
- Milfa: Broad Buster (BB) from Grancista; Zenon fan; joins ev_21, leaves ev_22, back for ev_23.
- Zenon: leader of the Zenon Warriors; REVEAL ev_19 / ev_1C: Beet's elder brother. Laio, Cruss, Bluezam, Alside: the
  other four; their Saiga live in Beet.
- Beltorze: "King of Tragedy"; killed the Zenon Warriors; the Redou one is a Phantom (REVEAL 1C5772).
- Grineed: "the Verdant Strategist", calm gentleman, true nature the Bloodstained Beast; lord of the Black Horizon.
- Dangol: Grineed's fawning monster aide; survives everything.
- Rozzgoat, Ventura, Frausky: Grineed's Vandels (5-star watcher; 3-star schemer; hitman "Vivid Crimson Bullet").
- Mugine: two-star Vandel of the Dead Swamp. Shaderico: "Fang of Darkness", bat Vandel. Helgar: Grineed's pet monster.
- Appraisers (鑑定士): Anklus = old woman ("Granny"); Redou = old man ("Gramps"); Terceron and the blob-quest town =
  old women; Patres = おっちゃん; Insua = polite. Match each town.
- Horus (Unorom Buster, Saiga mentor), Juno (tutorial Buster), Debre (Anklus elder), Fural (Anklus youth), Haia
  (Insua elder), Athos (Elfast armorer), Wes (Primore researcher, woman), Minny (Redou waitress), Media (lighthouse
  keeper), Mac and Defi (survey Busters), Alford (Trowana soldier, killed by Frausky), Kou / Shiho / Dr. Yuzu,
  Shin / Ayano / Buena / Tink (Etria family), Philip (Terceron), Goran / Shiba / Grest / Badu (recruitable
  mercenaries), Cabin (Terceron merchant), Gazetta (Dawn Axe boss), Orfina (ancient-script scholar), the King of
  Trowana.
- The Gate (門): each village's living gate; it talks, feels, can't smell. The Mysterious Voice: sanctuary voice.
